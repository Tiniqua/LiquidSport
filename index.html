Be Liquid

Reality is where we live, so let’s get good at reality

Nerves, mistakes, dips in confidence, uncertainty, and setbacks are constant. All that matters is that we manage ourselves.

A lot of performance mindset advice starts with where you want to be: be confident, think positively, and believe in yourself. But what if that feels out of reach and disconnected from where you are right now? We reverse engineer the process and examine the experience in real terms. It is hard, but here is what we can do.

Body. Breathe. Brain.

A simple and practical way to manage yourself when the pressure hits.

Body

Manage your body language to influence your presence in the moment.

Breathe

Manage your breathing to regulate yourself.

Brain

Think clearly about your next step.

Life will not follow our neatly made plans

  • We get nervous
  • We make mistakes
  • We feel pressure
  • We doubt ourselves

That is reality. A Liquid Mindset is about getting better at managing our way through it. Yes, it is hard. So, what are we going to do about it?
